Concrete steps are a safety item as much as a building element. When nosings chip, risers go uneven or the whole flight starts to rock, every trip to the door carries a little risk. Concrete Toronto repairs and replaces concrete steps across Toronto and the GTA, front entrances and back doors alike.

Steps fail from the edges in. Water sits on the tread, frost works on the nosing, and de-icing salt accelerates the surface breakdown. We form new steps with consistent risers, proper tread depth and a broom finish for grip, and we rebuild the base so the flight does not settle out of level.

What's included in our concrete step service

Every step job is priced as a complete package in a written quote:

  • An honest assessment: repair where the structure is sound, replace where it is not
  • Removal and disposal of the old steps where replacement is needed
  • Base preparation so the new flight stays level
  • Consistent risers and full-depth treads formed to your door height
  • A broom finish for grip in wet and icy weather
  • Full cleanup and curing guidance before we leave

Part of the whole entrance

Steps rarely exist alone. They meet a porch at the top and a walkway at the bottom, and the step sides often take a parge coat for a clean finish. Our crew handles all of it in one visit, one quote and one schedule.
