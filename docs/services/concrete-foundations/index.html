Everything above a foundation depends on it, which is why we treat foundation work as the most serious concrete we pour. Concrete Toronto handles residential and commercial foundation projects across Toronto and the GTA: additions, garages, and foundations and parking spaces for businesses.

As a contractor that started in landscaping and construction, we know the ground decides how a foundation behaves. Soil bearing, drainage and frost depth all shape the design, and our crews prepare every site with that in mind before a form goes in.

What's included in our concrete foundation service

Foundation jobs are scoped individually, but every quote covers the work end to end:

  • Consultation and site evaluation, including soil and drainage conditions
  • Design and planning with measurements, layout and material selection
  • Excavation coordination and site preparation
  • Forming, pouring and finishing to the engineered spec
  • Waterproofing to protect the foundation from water damage
  • Parging for a protected, finished surface above grade
  • Final inspection and clean-up before we hand the site over

Waterproofing and parging

Concrete is strong, but water is patient. Our parging service protects the exposed band of foundation above grade, and our waterproofing protects it below. Together they keep moisture off the wall and out of the basement, which matters in Toronto's freeze-thaw climate more than almost anywhere.

Compliant, inspected work

Foundation work is regulated work. Our contractors know Toronto's by-laws and building requirements, and we make sure your project complies with local regulations from the first sketch to the final inspection.
