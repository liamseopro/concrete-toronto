The porch is the handshake of the house, and it takes a beating: foot traffic every day, water off the roof line, and freeze-thaw all winter. Concrete Toronto repairs, resurfaces and pours concrete porches across Toronto and the GTA.

Porch problems usually start at the base or the joint where the porch meets the house. Settlement opens a gap, water gets in, frost pries it wider, and within a few winters the surface is spalling and the edge is crumbling. When we rebuild, we fix the base first so the same cycle does not start over.

What's included in our concrete porch service

Every porch job is priced as a complete package in a written quote:

  • An honest assessment: resurface if the structure is sound, rebuild if it is not
  • Excavation and a compacted base that addresses settlement
  • Forming to the right height so the porch meets your door threshold properly
  • Pour and finish, broom texture for grip
  • Steps and landings where the entrance needs them
  • Full cleanup and curing guidance before we leave

Porches, steps and parging in one visit

A porch job often pairs naturally with new steps and a fresh parge coat on the porch base or foundation wall beside it. One crew, one quote, one schedule, and the whole entrance looks finished at once.
