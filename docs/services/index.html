Installation, repair or replacement?

The right call depends on the condition of what is there now. Hairline cracks and small spalled patches are usually a repair. When cracks run the full width of a slab, sections have heaved or sunk, or the surface is crumbling across most of its area, replacement is the smarter spend. And if the concrete is bare or was never poured, that is a straight installation. We will give you an honest read when we quote, because a repair on concrete that needs replacing wastes your money, and replacing concrete that only needs a repair wastes even more.

Whatever the job, the fundamentals do not change: proper site preparation, a stable base, the right mix for Ontario freeze-thaw conditions, a careful finish and enough cure time. That discipline is what our 2-year warranty is built on. We serve the whole GTA, so see the full list of areas we serve if you are outside the city.
