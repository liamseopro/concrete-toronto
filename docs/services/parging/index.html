Parging is the thin cement-based coat on the exposed band of your foundation, and it does two jobs at once: it shields the wall from moisture and weather, and it gives the base of the building a clean, finished look. Concrete Toronto parges walls, basements and more across Toronto and the GTA, keeping your buildings protected and presentable.

When a parge coat starts to crack, flake or bubble, the wall underneath takes the weather directly. Water works in behind the loose coat, frost pries it further, and by spring the patches have become sheets. Catching it early is a small, quick job. Waiting means more prep and a bigger repair.

What's included in our parging service

Every parging job is priced as a complete package in a written quote:

  • Foundation wall parging on the exposed band above grade
  • Basement parging for interior walls that need a clean, sealed finish
  • Parging repair for cracks, flaking and sections that have let go
  • Proper prep: loose material removed, surface cleaned, coat bonded so it stays put
  • Waterproofing to protect concrete foundations from water damage
  • Full cleanup when the work is done

Parging and waterproofing together

Parging sheds surface water, but it is not a substitute for waterproofing where water is actively getting in. We provide waterproofing services to protect concrete foundations from water damage, and we will tell you plainly which one your wall needs. Often the right answer is both: waterproof where the water pressure is, parge where the wall shows.

Parging questions, answered

Parging is a thin coat of cement-based mortar applied to the exposed part of a foundation or masonry wall. It creates a smooth, uniform surface that protects against moisture and weather while giving the building a clean, finished look.
We parge walls, basements and more: exposed foundation walls above grade, interior basement walls, porch bases and other masonry surfaces that need protection or a tidier finish.
