A walkway is a small surface with a big job: it guides every visitor to your door and it has to stay even and safe through decades of freeze-thaw. Concrete Toronto pours front, side and backyard walkways across Toronto and the GTA, straight runs and gentle curves alike.

Walkways fail when water gets under them. We excavate to a proper depth, compact a granular base, and grade the surface so water sheds to the side instead of pooling or running at your foundation. That prep is what keeps the joints from heaving when the frost comes.

What's included in our concrete walkway service

Every walkway job is priced as a complete package in a written quote:

  • Layout and design, straight or curved to suit the yard
  • Removal of the old walkway where there is one, with disposal included
  • Excavation and a compacted granular base
  • A broom finish for grip in wet and icy weather
  • Control joints placed to keep any shrinkage cracking where we planned it
  • Full cleanup when the job is done

Walkways, sidewalks, steps, done together

Front walkways usually meet a sidewalk at one end and steps at the other. If any of those need work, bundling them into one visit means one quote and one schedule, and the finishes match. We will price the bundle honestly: if a section only needs a repair, we say so.

Concrete walkway questions, answered

Three feet is the practical minimum for one person, and four feet lets two people walk side by side. We measure your lot and recommend a width that fits the entrance and the frontage.
Yes. Concrete forms can be set to gentle curves, which often suits front yards better than a straight run. We lay out the line with you on site before any forms go in.
A broom finish. The fine texture gives grip under snow and ice, which is why we recommend it for walkways. Smooth trowel finishes look sleek but get slippery when wet.
If one section has lifted at a joint, a targeted repair can work. If several sections are out of level or the surface is crumbling along its length, replacement gives you a walkway that stays even. We will tell you honestly which one yours needs.
A typical front walkway is a one to two day job once we start: excavation and base on day one, pour and finish shortly after. We confirm the schedule in writing before work begins, and we stick to it.
