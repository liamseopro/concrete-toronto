Some concrete is simply done. When cracks run the full width of a slab, sections have heaved or sunk, or the surface is crumbling wherever you look, patching is money spent delaying the inevitable. Concrete Toronto removes old surfaces and installs fresh, new concrete for a clean, modern look, across Toronto and the GTA.

Replacement is also the chance to fix what caused the failure. We regrade so water drains where it should, and we build a compacted base sized for the load. The new surface starts life on a better foundation than the old one ever had.

What's included in our concrete replacement service

Every replacement job is priced as a complete package in a written quote:

  • Break-out and haul-away of the old concrete or asphalt
  • Excavation and regrading for proper drainage
  • A compacted granular base built for freeze-thaw conditions
  • Forms, pour and finish, broom texture or smooth where you want it
  • Control joints placed to manage shrinkage
  • Barricading, cleanup and clear curing guidance

Repair or replace: the honest test

We quote both when both are on the table. Isolated damage on sound concrete is a repair. Widespread failure is a replacement. The difference in price is real, so we show you the condition of your surface and let the evidence make the case.
