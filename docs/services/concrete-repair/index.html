Not every tired surface needs to come out. When the concrete underneath is sound, a proper repair restores the space for a fraction of the cost of a new pour. Concrete Toronto repairs and resurfaces concrete cracks and uneven surfaces across Toronto and the GTA.

The key word is proper. A patch slapped over a dirty, loose surface fails within a season. We cut out the failed material, clean the substrate, and bond the repair so it becomes part of the slab instead of sitting on top of it.

What's included in our concrete repair service

Every repair is quoted in writing after we see the damage:

  • An honest assessment of repair versus replacement
  • Crack repair for driveways, walkways, sidewalks and slabs
  • Resurfacing for scaled, spalled or pitted areas
  • Edge and nosing rebuilds on steps and curbs
  • Levelling of uneven sections where the base allows it
  • Full cleanup when the work is done

When repair is the wrong answer

We will not sell you a repair that cannot last. If the base has failed, if cracking runs through the slab, or if the surface is gone across most of the area, replacement is the honest recommendation and we will show you why. A repair on concrete that needs replacing just postpones the inevitable and costs you twice.

Concrete repair questions, answered

Cracks, surface scaling and spalling, chipped edges and nosings, and uneven sections. We repair and resurface concrete cracks and uneven surfaces to restore the space.
Isolated cracks and surface wear on otherwise sound concrete are repair jobs. Cracks running the full width of a slab, sections that have heaved or sunk, or surface failure across most of the area point to replacement. We will tell you honestly which one you have.
New repair material cures lighter and darkens as it weathers, so a fresh repair stands out at first and blends over a few seasons. Where the contrast would bother you, resurfacing a larger area gives a uniform look from day one.
Done on sound concrete with proper prep, a repair lasts for years, and our work carries the same 2-year warranty as our new pours. Prep is where cheap repairs fail, and it is where we spend the time.
