A sidewalk has one job: give people a safe, even path, in January ice and July heat alike. Concrete Toronto pours and replaces concrete sidewalks for homes and businesses across Toronto and the GTA, and we build them to stay level through the freeze-thaw cycle.

Sidewalk work in Toronto comes with rules. Widths, grades and curb details are covered by local by-laws, and our contractors know them. We make sure your project complies with local regulations, and if a permit or inspection applies, we tell you before work starts, not after.

What's included in our concrete sidewalk service

Every sidewalk job is priced as a complete package in a written quote:

  • Removal and disposal of broken or heaved sections
  • Excavation and a compacted granular base to resist frost movement
  • By-law compliant widths and grades for Toronto sidewalks
  • A broom finish for grip in wet and icy weather
  • Control joints tooled to keep sections even
  • Barricading and cleanup so the path stays safe while it cures

Section replacement or the whole run?

Trip hazards usually show up at joints, where one flag has lifted above its neighbour. If the rest of the walk is sound, we can replace just the damaged sections. When heaving and surface scaling run the length of the sidewalk, full replacement is the better value. We will show you both prices and let you decide.

Commercial sidewalks too

We handle commercial concrete jobs: sidewalks, parking spaces and other structures for businesses. If your storefront or property needs a safe, compliant path, the same crew and the same 2-year warranty apply.
