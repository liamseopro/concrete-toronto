A good patio turns the backyard into usable living space, and concrete is the practical way to build it. It is durable, low-maintenance and cost-effective compared to most other materials, and it handles patio furniture, grills and freeze-thaw winters without complaint. Concrete Toronto designs and pours concrete patios across Toronto and the GTA.

The two things that decide how a patio ages are the base and the slope. We excavate, compact a granular base, and grade the surface so water runs away from the house. Skip either one and you get pooling water in summer and heaving slabs in winter. We do not skip either one.

What's included in our concrete patio service

Every patio job is priced as a complete package in a written quote:

  • Design and layout based on how you use the yard, with measurements and material selection
  • Excavation and a compacted granular base built for freeze-thaw conditions
  • Proper slope so meltwater and rain drain away from your foundation
  • Forms, pour and finish, broom texture or smooth trowel where you want it
  • Control joints placed to manage natural shrinkage
  • Full cleanup and clear curing guidance before we leave

Pair it with the rest of the yard

A patio rarely stands alone. Most of our patio jobs connect to a walkway, a set of steps down from a door, or a fresh look at the foundation wall behind it. Because our crew handles all of it, you get one quote, one schedule and one cleanup instead of coordinating three trades.

Good to know

Concrete patio questions, answered

Think about how you will use it. A table and four chairs needs roughly 12 by 12 feet, and a lounge area with a grill wants more. During the consultation we measure the yard, talk through how you use the space, and recommend a size and layout that fits both.
All concrete can develop hairline cracks, but the big failures come from poor prep. We compact a granular base, slope the patio to shed water away from the house, and place control joints so any shrinkage cracking happens where we planned it.
A broom or light texture finish gives the best footing when the surface is wet or icy. We can also add simple borders or a smoother trowel finish in seating areas. We will show you options during the quote visit.
Foot traffic is fine after 24 to 48 hours. Give it about a week before heavy furniture and a full month before the heaviest loads, while the concrete reaches full strength.
