A concrete driveway works harder than almost any other surface on your property. It carries your vehicles every single day, takes shovels and road salt all winter, and it is the first thing people see when they pull up to your home. Concrete Toronto pours driveways across Toronto and the GTA, from single-car pads to wide double drives, and we build every one of them to stand up to Ontario weather.

Most driveways that fail early do not fail because of the concrete. They fail because of what is underneath. A slab poured over loose or poorly drained fill will crack, heave and settle within a few winters. That is why we put as much work into excavation and base compaction as we put into the pour itself. Get the ground right and the surface above it stays flat and solid for decades.

What's included in our concrete driveway service

Every driveway job is priced as a complete package in a written quote, so nothing changes partway through. Here is what we take care of:

  • Removal and disposal of your old concrete or asphalt driveway
  • Excavation and grading so water drains away from the house, not toward it
  • A compacted granular base, the single biggest factor in how long your driveway lasts
  • Concrete mixed and poured for Ontario freeze-thaw conditions
  • Your choice of finish, with broom texture recommended for winter grip
  • Control joints cut or tooled in the right places to manage natural shrinkage
  • Full cleanup plus clear guidance on curing and sealing

Signs your driveway is due for replacement

Hairline surface cracks are normal on older concrete and usually nothing to worry about. The warning signs we look for are wider cracks that run the full width of a slab, sections that have sunk or heaved out of line, crumbling across the surface, and pitting from years of road salt. If the damage is limited to one corner, a repair can make sense. When it shows up everywhere you look, replacement is usually the smarter spend.

Drainage is the other thing worth checking. A driveway that has settled toward the house sends meltwater straight at your foundation walls, and that often shows up later as damp basement corners. If we spot that pattern during your quote visit, we will say so, since a properly graded new driveway and parging often go hand in hand.
