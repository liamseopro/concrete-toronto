Richmond Hill has a bit of everything for a concrete crew: mature streets off Yonge with driveways poured decades ago, newer subdivisions up toward Elgin Mills with flatwork that is aging faster than it should, and plenty of sloped yards that need careful grading before a patio or walkway goes in. Concrete Toronto works across all of it, and we back each job with a 2-year warranty.

Grading matters more here than most places. On a sloped lot, water that runs the wrong way ends up at your foundation. We grade every driveway, patio and walkway to shed water away from the house, and we build the compacted base that keeps the surface level through York Region's freeze-thaw winters.

  • Backyard patios, including sloped-yard grading and steps
  • Walkways and sidewalk sections

Matching what is already there

A common Richmond Hill job is adding a walkway or patio beside concrete that is staying. We match finish and layout so the new work reads as part of the same property, and we are upfront about the cure: fresh concrete starts lighter and darkens as it weathers, blending further over the first few seasons.
