Concrete Toronto is based in Toronto, and our service areas cover the city plus the GTA communities around it. The same crew that pours a driveway in North York on Tuesday is replacing a walkway in Pickering on Thursday, and our 2-year warranty travels with us to every job. If your city is on this page, you get the same free quote, the same materials and the same finish we would put on a house on our own street.

Covering this much ground keeps us honest about how different the housing stock is from one city to the next. Downtown Toronto has century homes on tight lots. Scarborough and North York are full of postwar bungalows whose original concrete has taken seventy winters of freeze and thaw. Vaughan, Markham and Richmond Hill lean newer, where builder-grade flatwork often needs attention within its first decade or two. We quote every job on what it actually needs, not on a template.

What we do in every city we serve

Everything on our services page is available in every area listed below. The calls we get most often:

  • Concrete driveways: new pours and full replacements on a compacted base
  • Patios, walkways and sidewalks: flatwork that stays level through freeze-thaw
  • Porches and steps: safe, solid entrances
  • Foundations and slabs: for additions, garages and commercial work
  • Repair, replacement and parging for surfaces and walls that need attention

Not sure which of those you need? Describe the project in the form, or snap a photo, and we will sort that out when we put your quote together.
