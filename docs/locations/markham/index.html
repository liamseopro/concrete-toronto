Markham is two cities in one for concrete work. The older streets around Unionville and Milliken have mature homes with driveways and walkways from the 70s and 80s that are reaching the end of their run. The newer subdivisions in Cornell, Wismer and the north end have the opposite problem: builder-grade flatwork poured thin over minimal base, already cracking and settling within its first decade or two. Concrete Toronto works across all of it, and we back each job with a 2-year warranty.

Both problems have the same root cause: what is underneath the concrete. When we replace a driveway or walkway, we excavate to a proper depth, compact a granular base, and pour a mix suited to York Region's freeze-thaw winters. The surface above a good base stays flat for decades.
