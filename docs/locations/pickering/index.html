Pickering straddles two eras of housing. The mature neighbourhoods near the waterfront and along Kingston Road have driveways and walkways with real age on them, while the newer communities up through Brock Ridge and toward Claremont are filling in yards that need patios, walkways and shed slabs for the first time. Concrete Toronto works across all of it, and we back each job with a 2-year warranty.

One honest note for new-build owners: fresh fill settles. If your yard was graded in the last year or two, we may tell you to let it sit a season before we pour. It is not the answer anyone wants, but it is the one that keeps your new patio level five years from now.
