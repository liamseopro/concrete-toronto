Scarborough is bungalow country. Postwar homes in Wexford, Bendale and Dorset Park sit on wide lots with double driveways poured decades ago, and after that many winters of salt and freeze-thaw, a lot of that original concrete is cracked, heaved or scaling. Down in Birch Cliff, Cliffside and Guildwood, homes near the Bluffs take wind and moisture off the lake all year, which is hard on any exterior surface. Concrete Toronto handles concrete work in Scarborough every week, and we back each job with a 2-year warranty.

The good news about Scarborough lots is room to work. Driveways are wide, side yards are accessible, and most jobs have space for proper excavation and base work. That matters, because the base is what decides whether your new driveway or walkway is still level in twenty years.

What we do on Scarborough streets

Scarborough calls tend to cluster around a few jobs:

  • Concrete driveways, replacements of original postwar pours and new driveways for renovated homes
  • Backyard patios and side walkways
  • Porches and front steps repaired, resurfaced or rebuilt
  • Sidewalk sections replaced where they have heaved
  • Parging for foundation walls, plus repair and replacement work

Every job starts with proper prep. We remove what has failed, compact a granular base, and pour a mix suited to our freeze-thaw climate. Prep is where cheap jobs cut corners, and it is why so much flatwork fails early. Ours is built to stay put.

Driveways, porches and steps, one visit

A lot of Scarborough homes need the same trio: driveway, front porch and steps. Because our crew handles all of it, you get one quote, one schedule and one cleanup, and the finishes match across the whole frontage. If your foundation coating is also flaking, we can add parging to the same visit.
