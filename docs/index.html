How it works

Our six-step installation process

Every project follows the same clear path, so you always know what happens next.

STEP 01

Initial Consultation

We discuss your vision, needs and budget, then inspect the site and give you our recommendations.

STEP 02

Design & Planning

We put together a detailed plan with measurements, layout and material selection.

STEP 03

Site Preparation

We clear the area, set the forms and build a stable base. Good prep is what makes concrete last.

STEP 04

Mixing & Pouring

We mix to the right consistency and pour evenly into the forms for a strong, resilient surface.

STEP 05

Finishing Touches

We smooth the surface, add texture or pattern where wanted, and let the concrete cure properly.

STEP 06

Inspection & Clean-Up

We walk the finished job with you, check every detail and leave the site clean.
